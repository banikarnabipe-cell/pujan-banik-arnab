02 / 06 · What deleted means

Deleting removes the order, not the bytes.

A photo on a card is a grid of bytes. Deleting it takes away the name and the order. The bytes stay until the space is reused.

IntactEvery byte in place. Name, folder and date included.
DeletedStill here. Retriever finds the pieces and puts them back in order.
OverwrittenGone. Nobody gets this back, and Retriever says so.

On FAT12, FAT16, FAT32, exFAT and NTFS, names, folders and dates come back too, also after a quick format where the old directories survived. Files deleted in the Finder often sit in the card's hidden trash, and those come back with names.

What it does not do

  • Bring back overwritten data.
  • Guess at a video when a piece lies out of order or was overwritten. It stays honestly marked "fragmented".
  • Repair damaged files.
  • Recover files from the Mac's own disk. Apple Silicon Macs keep it encrypted, so a scan would find only cipher text. For that, look in Photos under Recently Deleted, or in Time Machine.
  • Read APFS, HFS+ or ext.
